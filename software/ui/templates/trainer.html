Step 3 · Set the learning conditions
Your AI is a giant list of numbers — like thousands of volume knobs. Each knob controls how much attention the AI pays to something it sees. Training slowly turns those knobs to the right positions.
Epochs (times through your data) 60
Your AI looks at every one of your examples, one by one. This number controls how many times it does that full pass.
Learning rate (how much each knob turns after a wrong guess)
Latent size (detail level) 128
How many features your AI tracks per image — things like color, edges, and shapes. Higher = more detail, but harder to learn.
Window size (reading chunk) 40
How many words your AI looks at before guessing what word comes next. Set to 40 means it reads 40 words at a time.
Batch Size
How many texts your AI studies at once.
Augmentation (practice variety) 50×
Takes each of your images and creates flipped, rotated, and recolored versions automatically. Gives your AI more examples to learn from without you uploading more photos.
Batch size (group size)
Your AI looks at this many images at once, then stops and adjusts itself. Smaller = adjusts more often. Larger = adjusts less often but more smoothly.
